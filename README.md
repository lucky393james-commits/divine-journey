# Divine Journey - A 3D Educational Action RPG

**Divine Journey** is an open-world educational action RPG inspired by Bhutan's rich history, geography, culture, environment, and traditional way of life.

## Overview

Players control a young hero who discovers ancient divine powers and embarks on a transformative journey across a fictionalized but educational version of Bhutan. Through exploration, puzzle-solving, and combat, players learn about:

- **Bhutanese History** - Discover ancient ruins, monasteries, dzongs, trade routes, and meet characters inspired by historical figures
- **Geography** - Navigate mountains, valleys, rivers, and learn about altitude, climate, and natural resources
- **Biodiversity & Pollination** - Protect ecosystems, restore habitats, and understand pollinator science
- **Culture & Tradition** - Experience Bhutanese way of life, architecture, and spiritual beliefs

## Game Features

### Gameplay Mechanics
- **Action-RPG Combat** - Engage corrupted guardian spirits in real-time battles
- **3D Exploration** - Travel across diverse regions with unique environmental challenges
- **Historical Quests** - Discover ancient manuscripts, solve historical mysteries, and restore cultural landmarks
- **Geographical Challenges** - Use maps, compasses, and navigation skills to explore
- **Environmental Restoration** - Complete pollination mini-games and habitat restoration activities

### Educational Systems
- **History Journal** - Records discovered events, places, people, and artifacts
- **Nature Journal** - Documents plants, animals, and ecological information
- **Interactive Map** - Shows mountains, rivers, settlements, roads, and landmarks
- **Divine Knowledge System** - Earn Knowledge Points through discoveries and quests to unlock abilities and areas

### Regions to Explore
1. **Sacred Valley** - Beginner area introducing basic geography and village life
2. **Whispering Forest** - Biodiversity and wildlife exploration
3. **Thunder Mountains** - Mountain climbing and altitude-based challenges
4. **Ancient Monastery** - Historical puzzles and artifact discovery
5. **River Kingdom** - Watershed and water resource management
6. **Corrupted Land** - Environmental restoration challenges
7. **Divine Realm** - Final challenge combining all learning elements

## Technology

- **Engine**: Godot 4.3
- **Language**: GDScript
- **Target Platforms**: PC, Console (planned)

## Getting Started

1. Clone this repository
2. Open the project in Godot 4.3
3. Navigate to the `scenes/main/` folder
4. Run `main.tscn` to start the game

## Educational Content

This game is designed to teach players about Bhutan through interactive gameplay, including:
- Real historical sites and figures
- Accurate geographical features and climate zones
- Native plant and animal species
- Bhutanese cultural traditions and values
- Environmental conservation principles
